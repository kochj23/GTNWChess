Stockfish 19 — bundled with GTNW Chess as a separate program
=============================================================

GTNW Chess ships an unmodified arm64 build of Stockfish 19 (Contents/MacOS/stockfish) and talks to it over the
UCI protocol through pipes. Stockfish is free software licensed under the GNU General Public License, version 3
(see COPYING.txt in this folder). The Stockfish authors are listed in AUTHORS.txt.

Corresponding source
--------------------
The exact source for the bundled binary is the official Stockfish 19 release:
  https://github.com/official-stockfish/Stockfish/tree/sf_19
  https://github.com/official-stockfish/Stockfish/archive/refs/tags/sf_19.tar.gz
The binary was built from that tag by Homebrew (formula "stockfish", version 19, bottle for arm64 macOS) with no
changes; the embedded NNUE networks are the ones referenced by that source tag.
